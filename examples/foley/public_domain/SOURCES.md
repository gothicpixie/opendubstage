# Example photographs: provenance

All five images are in the public domain. Each was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons as a 1600 px wide thumbnail on 2026-10-12, and the licence field was read from the Commons API (`LicenseShortName: Public domain`).

Four are US Farm Security Administration photographs, which are works of the US federal government held by the Library of Congress. The fifth is an 1890s Photochrom print from the Library of Congress Photochrom collection.

The captions in `sequence.json` are paraphrased from the catalogue titles. They stand in for the catalogue records a documentary would normally have.

## Why these five were chosen

Each picture exercises a different rule:

- **The train** gets a distant texture.
- **The street** gets a spot (a car far away) over a city bed.
- **Migrant Mother** is a portrait. It gets the bed only, and it sits under the demo's key line.
- **Pie Town** gets a car spot that the planner *rejects*, because the same sound class was used too recently and the key-line breath is still running. The picture actually shows a horse team, which also exposes the limits of keyword and label analysis.
- **The lake** gets a water texture over a different chapter's bed, crossfaded.
